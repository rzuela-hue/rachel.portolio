Goals and Reflections

My Career Goal in 5 Years

In five years, I want to be a graduate or a soon-to-be graduate of Information Systems who can build and manage useful systems for a company. I want to be skilled in web development and databases, and to work in a team where I can keep learning.

How I Use Technology Every Day

  • I use social media to stay connected with friends and family.
  • I use Google Docs, Word and PowerPoint for school work.
  • I use VS Code to practice making websites.

A Risk of Technology

One risk is spending too much time on social media, which can waste study time. I handle it by setting time for my school tasks first and using my phone after.

3 Lessons I Learned in This Course

  1. Big websites start with small, simple code.
  2. Mistakes are normal, and testing helps me find them.
  3. Planning my work saves time.

How I Learn Best

I learn best by trying things myself and looking at examples. From my teacher, I need clear instructions and feedback when I am stuck.
